# Phase 5: Certification Audit Support

> **Directory:** `05-certification/` | **Internal Reference**

---

## What This Phase Accomplishes

Certification audit support covers the period from engaging the certification body through receiving the certification decision. Your role in this phase shifts from builder to advisor. The client owns the audit process; you're there to help them prepare, stay calm, and respond effectively to auditor findings. This phase spans two stages: Stage 1 is a documentation review where the auditor confirms the AIMS is ready for Stage 2; Stage 2 is the full conformity assessment where the auditor evaluates whether the AIMS is implemented and effective. Nonconformities raised at either stage require written responses and evidence of corrective action before certification is granted.

## Phase Inputs

Before starting Phase 5, you need:

- Completed Pre-Audit Readiness Checklist from Phase 4 (signed off)
- Internal audit completed with findings addressed
- Management review completed and documented
- Certification body selected and Stage 1 date confirmed
- All AIMS documentation finalized and version-controlled

---

## Phase Outputs

Phase 5 produces:

- **Stage 1 response** (if the auditor raises clarifications or minor points) — written responses to any Stage 1 findings
- **Stage 2 nonconformity responses** (if applicable) — documented corrective actions with evidence, submitted to the certification body within the agreed timeframe
- **Certification** — the ISO/IEC 42001:2023 certificate, typically valid for three years with annual surveillance audits

---

## Estimated Duration

**2–4 weeks** of active consultant involvement, though the overall timeline depends on certification body scheduling. Stage 1 and Stage 2 are typically separated by 4–8 weeks. Nonconformity response periods add additional time if findings are raised.

---

## Key ISO 42001 Clauses Addressed

The certification audit covers the full standard. Auditors typically focus most attention on:

| Clause | Topic |
|--------|-------|
| 4.3 | Scope of the AIMS |
| 5.2 | AI policy |
| 6.1.2–6.1.3 | Risk assessment and treatment |
| 8.4 | AI system impact assessment |
| 9.2–9.3 | Internal audit and management review |
| 10.2 | Nonconformity and corrective action |
| Annex A (A.2–A.10) | Control implementation evidence |
