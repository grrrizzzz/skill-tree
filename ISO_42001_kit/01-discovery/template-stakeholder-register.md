# Stakeholder Register — AI Management System

> **Current as of:** March 2026 | ISO/IEC 42001:2023

> **Document type:** Client Template — Complete and maintain as part of your AIMS documentation

---

## Purpose

This register identifies and documents all interested parties relevant to your organization's AI Management System (AIMS), as required by ISO/IEC 42001:2023, Clause 4.2.

Interested parties are persons or organizations that can affect, be affected by, or perceive themselves to be affected by your organization's AI-related decisions and activities. This includes both internal stakeholders (employees, management, board) and external stakeholders (regulators, customers, AI vendors, data subjects).

Maintaining this register ensures your AIMS accounts for everyone who has a stake in how your organization uses AI. It is a living document — review and update it whenever your AI landscape, organizational structure, or regulatory environment changes.

---

## How to Complete This Register

1. **Start with the pre-filled examples below.** These represent the most common stakeholder types for organizations that use third-party AI systems. Review each example and determine whether it applies to your organization.

2. **Adapt the examples to your context.** Replace generic descriptions with specifics. For example, change "AI SaaS Vendors" to your actual vendor names. Adjust priority levels based on your organization's circumstances.

3. **Add any stakeholders unique to your organization** that are not covered by the examples. Consider your industry, geographic locations, contractual relationships, and regulatory environment.

4. **For each stakeholder, complete all columns:**
   - **Stakeholder:** The person, group, or organization
   - **Category:** Internal or External
   - **Interest/Concern:** What they care about regarding your AI use
   - **Requirements:** What they need or expect from your AIMS (link to specific obligations where possible)
   - **Engagement Method:** How you will communicate with and involve this stakeholder
   - **Priority:** High (active engagement required), Medium (regular communication), or Low (inform as needed)
   - **ISO 42001 Reference:** The clause or Annex A control that makes this stakeholder relevant

5. **Review this register at least annually** and after any significant change to your AI systems, organizational structure, or regulatory requirements (see Clause 4.2 review triggers below).

## Clause 4.2 Requirements Mapping

ISO/IEC 42001:2023, Clause 4.2 requires your organization to determine:

| Requirement | What to Document | Where in This Register |
|---|---|---|
| **The interested parties that are relevant to the AIMS** | Every person, group, or organization with a stake in your AI activities | Stakeholder column — list each identified party |
| **The relevant requirements of those interested parties** | What each stakeholder needs, expects, or demands regarding your AI use | Requirements column — translate interests into specific, actionable requirements |
| **Which of these requirements will be addressed through the AIMS** | Confirm that your AIMS scope and controls address the documented requirements | ISO 42001 Reference column — map each requirement to the clause or Annex A control that addresses it |

### Completeness Check

- [ ] Customers / clients

- [ ] Legal counsel
- [ ] Business partners and suppliers
- [ ] Industry bodies or professional associations (if relevant)
- [ ] Investors or shareholders (if applicable)
- [ ] Insurance providers (if AI-related coverage exists)

## Review and Maintenance

This register should be reviewed and updated:

- **At least annually** as part of the management review process (Clause 9.3)
- **When new AI systems are adopted** or existing AI systems are retired
- **When organizational changes occur** (restructuring, mergers, new markets, new jurisdictions)
- **When regulatory requirements change** (new AI legislation, updated sector guidance)
- **When new interested parties are identified** through operational experience, incidents, or external feedback
- **Before certification audits** to ensure the register reflects the current state

Each review should be recorded with the date, reviewer name, and a summary of changes made.
