# Phase 3: Remediation & Implementation Support

> **Directory:** `03-remediation/` | **Internal Reference**

---

## What This Phase Accomplishes

Remediation is the longest and most intensive phase of the engagement. This is where the client actually builds their AIMS. Working from the prioritized gap register produced in Phase 2, you guide the client through creating the policies, procedures, assessments, and records that ISO 42001 requires. For most SMB clients, this means drafting documents they've never had before, not updating existing ones. The templates in this directory cover every major AIMS document type. The internal procedure explains how to sequence the work, manage client effort, and validate that completed documents actually meet the standard's requirements.

## Phase Inputs

Before starting Phase 3, you need:

- Completed and signed-off Gap Analysis Report from Phase 2
- Prioritized gap list with agreed remediation sequence
- Client project team identified (who will own each document)
- Client leadership commitment to the time and resources required

---

## Phase Outputs

Phase 3 produces the client's complete AIMS documentation set:

- **AI Policy** — top-level policy signed by leadership
- **Acceptable Use Policy** — operational rules for AI system use
- **Risk Assessment Register** — documented AI risks with scores
- **Impact Assessments** — per-system assessments of potential harms
- **Statement of Applicability** — control applicability decisions
- **Risk Treatment Plan** — treatment decisions and actions
- **Supplier Assessments** — vendor due diligence records
- **Training and Competence Records** — evidence of staff training
- **NCAR Log** — nonconformity and corrective action records
- **Implementation Roadmap** — milestone tracker for remaining work

---

## Estimated Duration

**6–12 weeks** for a typical SMB engagement. Duration depends heavily on the number of AI systems in scope, the client's internal capacity to contribute, and how many gaps were identified in Phase 2.

Clients with limited internal bandwidth are the most common cause of delays. Set clear expectations about client effort at the Phase 3 kickoff. The implementation roadmap template helps make those expectations concrete.
